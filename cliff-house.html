A terrace made for the last light.

Imagine pale stone, linen that lifts in the breeze and a long table set beneath a simple pergola. The horizon does most of the decorating here. Mornings begin with fruit and coffee; afternoons wander between the shade and the water below.

These illustrated notes are about the feeling of a home by the water. The house and setting are imagined, made for looking at slowly.
