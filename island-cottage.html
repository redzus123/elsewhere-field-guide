Tucked into a green pocket just above the beach, this little timber cottage lets the island set the schedule: birdsong at dawn, bare feet by noon, rain on the roof after dark.

A little daydream

A little house in the green.

Weathered wood, a deep veranda and a hammock that always seems to be in the shade. The sea is only a sandy path away, but the cottage makes staying put feel like an outing of its own. Bring a book, then forget to open it.

These illustrated notes are about the feeling of a home by the water. The house and setting are imagined, made for looking at slowly.
